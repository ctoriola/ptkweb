{ABOUT PRIMETEK}

Securing People. Protecting Assets. Strengthening Resilience.

PrimeTEK Safety & Security Consultants Ltd is a security and defence solutions company helping organisations, institutions and communities anticipate threats, manage risk and build stronger, more resilient security environments.

We bring together strategic consulting, intelligence, technology, surveillance, protective security, cybersecurity, training, emergency preparedness and specialised operational capabilities.

Our approach is built around a simple principle: effective security begins with understanding the threat. We work closely with our clients to understand their environment, identify vulnerabilities, assess risks and develop practical security solutions for their specific needs.

{WHO WE ARE}

A Security Partner Built Around Intelligence and Preparedness

Security challenges are becoming increasingly complex. Organisations must contend with physical threats, cyber risks, evolving criminal activity, vulnerabilities within critical infrastructure and rapidly changing operational environments.

At PrimeTEK, security goes beyond guards, equipment or isolated measures. Our model brings together people, intelligence, technology, processes and preparedness to create solutions that are comprehensive, responsive and adaptable.

Our capabilities span intelligence and counterintelligence, executive and VIP protection, critical infrastructure protection, cybersecurity, surveillance and reconnaissance, smart security technologies, training and simulation, disaster response and specialised security consulting.
